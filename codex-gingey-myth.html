Locked canon · Gingey / Aethernox

The Myth of the Void Sovereign

LOCKED CANON. No living mortal has ever seen the true Aethernox—not because he hides, but because whenever he appears, he is simply Gingey: the traveler, the bridge-builder, the stranger feeding birds. Across millions of years, history gave way to myth.

Every Religion Has Its Own Version

Every civilization depicts the Void Sovereign differently: faceless judge, six-winged cosmic monarch, living galaxy, dragon, child, woman, destroyer of worlds. None are wholly correct, yet each preserves a fragment of truth.

Luxen's Collection

Luxen maintains The 10,000 Incorrect Depictions of Lord Aethernox, a humorous archive that Aethernox himself enjoys rating for creativity.

Philosophy

“Because they don't need me. The moment people know exactly who their protector is, they stop believing they can protect one another. I'd rather they remember kindness than my face.”

The Legendary Reveal

For hundreds of chapters Gingey appears only as an ordinary traveler. When an ancient enemy demands the Void Sovereign, Gingey quietly sets down his teacup and answers: “You were looking for me?”
