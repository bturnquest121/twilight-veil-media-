Birthright = inherited power · Blessing = given power · Sentinel = recognized power. Similar names and abilities do not prove kinship. Forged records document deception, not reliable genealogy.

                                                            Solar, Frost and Blood separation; documented generations.

                                                            Displacement, lost records and identity changes; preserve Hollow Family roles.
                                                            VI · Titanfall
                                                            Vantari, Elven, Meridian, technical and healing-network candidates.
                                                            VII · Crossroads
                                                            Restoration registries, independent forges, roadkeepers and archive witnesses.
                                                            VIII · The Walking Star
                                                            Ahk Ra royal relatives, scattered lineages, Sun-Serpent and Eclipse Registry.
                                                            IX · Red Autumn
                                                            Ancient Kynari losses and dynastic records; later Observer account separate.
                                                            X · Pale Choir
                                                            Veyr, Blood Renewal, Ashveil and clandestine archive families.
                                                            XI · Parents’ era
