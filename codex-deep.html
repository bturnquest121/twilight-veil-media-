The Void Age · before creation

Before the First Star

eons before The Awakening

Aethernox — the boundary of existence, the permission that lets shape take shape — meets creation, is feared, worshipped, and shot at across the Three Answers, then spends ten thousand years shrinking himself small. A theologian counterfeits his "attention" and forges the Void Legion; rather than descend and end it, Aethernox picks up his bag, out-neighbours the war, and takes a harmless name.

The Ashcroft Crossing · the Veil's first children

The Hollow Family Is Born

long before the Nomads, Pharaohs, Elven Dynasties, or Kynari Packs

Nearly three hundred explorers, scholars, priests, families and refugees of the Ashcroft Expedition cross a permanent Tear — and the Tear seals behind them. They do not starve, and they do not die: their Anima exhausts itself, and the Veil rewrites them into the first beings to metabolize souls instead of food. The five who remain — the Mother, the Father, the Observer, the Lantern, the Twins — become the Veil's oldest myth, and the reason the Nomad Oath exists.

The Elder Wilds & the First Veil Collapse

The Age of Concealment Cultures

≈ 300 years before The Awakening (and earlier)

Vael'Thyr, an Elder-Wilds civilization older than the Elven Archives, is consumed beneath the Veil (the First Veil Collapse) — its child Elara (now 183) takes the road-name Ashveil and walks out. The Lunari, second-light weavers, teach the human Moon-Ward lineages, then Withdraw when their gift, the Pale Choir, is re-bound as a weapon. In the Blood realms, the Crimson Divide splits the Sovereign Clan and Adair walks out the unlocked gate to spend three centuries writing the Grand Regional Archive.

The Vantari Era

The Storm Under Doctrine

≈ 30 → 9 years before The Awakening

In the Spire-Halls, the Order calibrates storm-children to "zero variance." Kirshino — the first green anomaly — breaks into permanent violet chain-breaking light, is "corrected" out of all record, and becomes a counsel-trained freedom-fighter. Nine years his junior, his brother Arelion is raised under the empty frame, taught the green is "a loan," and calibrated into the most precise lightning-wielder in nine centuries.

The Recent Window · the living cast

The Twenty-Two Years

≈ 22 years before → the eve of The Awakening

Every present-day teammate's origin book crests in this window and delivers them, within months, to Cell 9. Riven is the clock's zero point: he is 19 at The Awakening, having spent the Nine Years (ages 0–9) hidden on Earth and the Long Chase (9–19) raised by grandparents while his parents played a decoy across the realms. The others arrive fresh from their own defining trials — Yahia 17, Scribs 17, Kaisel 17, Amelia 152, Nia 20, Asha 21, Sanaa 22, Vaughn ~20, Arelion 22.
