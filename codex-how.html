    How to read this codex

    The Two Timescales

    These characters are born centuries — sometimes eons — apart, yet they all arrive at one street in one present. To keep a coherent timeline, the codex runs on two clocks that meet at The Awakening (the present-day "Year 0," when Riven consciously calls the blue flame and the story proper begins).

    Clock One

    ⧗ The Deep Timeline

    Backstory time — measured before the present. Spans from before the first star (Gingey) through the Elder-Wilds collapse and the Vantari era down to the recent 22-year window that raised the living cast. Each origin book lives here.

    Clock Two

    ◷ The Story Spine

    Present time — measured in chapters. One continuous run, Ch. 1–140, across four seasons and eighteen arcs, on Earth (Charlotte) and the Veil beyond it. This is where every backstory converges.

    ⟡ Anchor: ages in the Deep Timeline are given as the character's own age or as "≈N years before The Awakening." The Story Spine uses continuous chapters: S1 Ch. 1–35 · S2 Ch. 36–70 · S3 Ch. 71–105 · S4 Ch. 106–140.
