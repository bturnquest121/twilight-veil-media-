The Pestilence Visual Companion — Collections 11–XLI

An illustrated atlas of Elohim-doctrine antagonists — Corrupted · Damned · Cursed · Madness. Plates 01–65 (the original 53-plate archive and the Tenth Collection) are preserved in Chapter IX of the Pestilence Codex above; this volume carries the field studies that followed: Collections 11 through XLI (with Collections XXXVI–XL pending), Plates 66–394, each an original design study with its cultural lineage, provisional faction, corruption class, relic and manifested power.

Archive contents. I. Individual character studies · II. Ensemble galleries and antagonistic faction plates · III. Corruption variants, rites, masks, sentinels and relic-bearers. Each image is preserved as a complete composition, without cutting up multi-character sheets. The ten outside visual references are excluded; this edition contains original generated concepts. Character names shown within artwork are concept labels, not automatically canonized lore. Every study is marked canon pending / concept entry only until ratified by the creative director.
