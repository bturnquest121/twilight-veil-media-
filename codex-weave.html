The long game

The Ancient Weave — Five Storylines

Five deep threads, each built to pay off a different wing of the cast. They braid toward one cosmological answer: the eclipse-marked client, the cut seams, the sleeping gods, and the compressing Veil are the same story.

Storyline 1 · S2→S3 hinge

The Buried Crown Beneath the Breathing God

Vashkarath is built in a sleeping god's ribcage; its beloved king wears a heart-crown that was never his while the true heir survives as a dockworker's daughter, Sef. Riven's mirror arc.

◆ Riven · Kaisel · Asha · Scribs
Storyline 2 · S2→S3 bridge

The City That Kept Him

Earth fog leads Vaughn to the Veilborn of the Grey Meridian — a second species inside the Veil who made him a citizen during his nine lost days, now compressed toward the surface by the same strain on the Charlotte seam.

◆ Vaughn · Riven · Gingey · Amelia
Storyline 3 · S2 back-half / S3

The Gallery of Corrected Kings

Two centuries of the Jackals editing noble bloodlines toward ownable lineages. The proof lives in Aurel's Gallery; its oldest resident, Tenoch, refuses rescue. The oldest ledger's mark is an eclipse, not a jackal.

◆ Kaisel · Ixtli · Riven
Storyline 4 · is the S2 Frozen-Sea arc

The Sea That Remembered Its Name

The "sea" is another Fallen God, listening to Vashkarath's. Class-Seven storms are dream-traffic; Yahia's dragon is a fragment of the sea-god, and the Frost-Reaper line are hereditary lullaby-keepers.

◆ Yahia · Sanaa · Gingey · Arelion · Riven
Storyline 5 · save entire for S3 spine

The Choir Under the Floor

The buried "accidents" were summons — a century of the Voss network calling to the Choir, a species beneath reality's floor, using stolen bloodline resonance as signal; Bellgrave's 41 missing names were the instrument. the outlawed Valequill seamstress lineage were the counter-force (orphaned by the Lyra→Amelia rework — reassignment pending); Riven's Pharaoh blood is the loudest signal ever mapped. The Choir answers testimony, not power.

◆ Sanaa · Asha · Riven · (seamstress thread: pending)
